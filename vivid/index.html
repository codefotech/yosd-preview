About YOSD

A youth organization working for a better life in its own community

To organize, train and mobilize young people so they can lead practical, community-based initiatives that improve lives and build a fairer, more resilient society.

  • Build the leadership, skills and confidence of young people.
  • Improve health, education and wellbeing in underserved communities.
  • Protect children and promote safe, inclusive spaces for all.

Our concern

We are focusing on four key areas

Health & Nutrition

Awareness sessions, health camps and nutrition support for families.

Education

Learning support and school retention for children and adolescents.

Youth Skills & Livelihood

Practical training that leads to income and employment.

Child Protection

Safeguarding children and preventing abuse, neglect and exploitation.
