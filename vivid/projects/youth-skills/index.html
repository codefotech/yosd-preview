Youth Skills & Livelihood

Short practical courses that help young people find work or start small businesses.

Skills & Livelihood Donor-funded
Objectives Initiatives Target group Achievements Reviews Photos Videos Reports

Project objectives

What this project aims to do

  • Raise awareness and knowledge in the target community.
  • Give direct, practical support to the people most in need.
  • Build the capacity of local youth volunteers to sustain the work.

Initiatives

What we do under this project

01

Community awareness sessions and courtyard meetings

02

Training workshops led by YOSD project leads

03

Service camps and door-to-door outreach

04

Follow-up visits and referral to local services

Target group

Who benefits

  • Children & adolescents
  • Youth volunteers
  • Women & mothers
  • Vulnerable families
