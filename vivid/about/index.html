About us

Young people, serving their community since 2015

Empowering Youth for Social Action and Sustainable Development.

Background

Our story

YOSD is a youth-led, non-profit organization based in Ukhia, Cox’s Bazar. Since 2015 we have been bringing young people together to serve their communities through social action and sustainable development.

The full background and history of YOSD, taken from the Organizational Profile, will appear here. It will explain how the organization began in Ukhia in 2015, who founded it, and how its work has grown over the years.

Type of organization
Youth-led, non-profit, voluntary organization
Based in
Ukhia, Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh

Our vision

“A just, inclusive and sustainable society where every young person has the power to lead change.”

Our mission

What we set out to do

To organize, train and mobilize young people so they can lead practical, community-based initiatives that improve lives and build a fairer, more resilient society.

Core values

The values that guide us

Youth leadership

Young people design, lead and own the work.

Integrity & accountability

We are open about what we do and how funds are used.

Inclusion & respect

Everyone is welcome, and everyone is treated with dignity.

Partnership

We work alongside communities, government and peers.

Sustainability

We aim for change that lasts beyond a single project.

Volunteerism

Service to the community is at the heart of YOSD.

Goals

What we want to achieve

  • Build the leadership, skills and confidence of young people.
  • Improve health, education and wellbeing in underserved communities.
  • Protect children and promote safe, inclusive spaces for all.
  • Encourage youth-led action on environment and climate.

Strategic objectives

How we will get there

  1. 1Run community projects in each thematic area every year.
  2. 2Train and certify young people through workshops and seminars.
  3. 3Grow an active network of members, volunteers and partners.
  4. 4Publish annual reports and financial information openly.
  5. 5Complete all national registrations and compliance requirements.
  6. 6Secure sustainable funding of BDT 5,00,000 per year.
