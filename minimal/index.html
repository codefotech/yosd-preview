What we do

Six areas. One community.

To organize, train and mobilize young people so they can lead practical, community-based initiatives that improve lives and build a fairer, more resilient society.

Our work
  1. 01 Health & NutritionAwareness sessions, health camps and nutrition support for families.
  2. 02 EducationLearning support and school retention for children and adolescents.
  3. 03 Youth Skills & LivelihoodPractical training that leads to income and employment.
  4. 04 Child ProtectionSafeguarding children and preventing abuse, neglect and exploitation.
  5. 05 Environment & ClimateTree planting, clean-ups and youth climate action.
  6. 06 Social CohesionDialogue and joint activities that bring communities together.

Our vision

A just, inclusive and sustainable society where every young person has the power to lead change.
